Pass The Phone

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · App ID: com.ahmedhassan.passthephone

Pass The Phone is an offline Egyptian Arabic party game. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

1. Information handled by the app

The app allows you to create custom game questions and saves your gameplay preferences, including theme, handoff-screen and vibration settings, surprise-card settings, and question rotation progress. This information is stored locally on your device for the game to work and remember your choices.

2. Collection, transmission, and sharing

In the current version described by this policy, the app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect or transmit your custom questions, gameplay settings, or question progress to our servers. The reviewed version does not integrate in-app advertising or third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share locally stored gameplay information with advertisers.

3. Device features and permissions

The app uses the Android vibration feature, when enabled in the game settings, to provide optional feedback during gameplay. It does not use vibration to gather personal information.

4. Storage, security, retention, and deletion

Custom questions, settings, and question rotation data are stored within the app's local storage, subject to your device's operating-system protections. They generally remain until removed through available app controls, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. You can delete individual custom questions from within the app. To remove all locally stored app information, use your Android device's app storage controls to clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Clearing data cannot be undone. Device backup and restore behavior depends on your device and Android settings.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account or online submission. The game includes different question categories; players and guardians should choose categories appropriate for their group. Any age-related store classification is provided separately through Google Play.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add features such as online accounts, analytics, advertising, or other data handling, we will review and update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate notices or obtain consent before those features are used.

7. Contact

Developer: Independent developer of Pass The Phone. For privacy questions or requests, please use the project's GitHub issue contact form. Note: GitHub issues are public; do not post personal or sensitive information there. A dedicated private support email will be added when available.
